# CrisisLens: what to study

Ten topics. Each ends with a question you should be able to answer in your own words once you've built that part. Paper links go to the arXiv abstract page (PDF linked there) or the publisher's page.

## Imagery

- **1. How satellites see water** (optical vs. radar, Sentinel-1 SAR backscatter, water indices). Why does radar see floods through monsoon clouds, and why does a flooded street sometimes look *brighter* than a dry one? *Read:* the [Sentinel-1 mission pages on SentiWiki](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s1-mission); UN-SPIDER's [recommended practice for flood mapping with Sentinel-1 in Google Earth Engine](https://www.un-spider.org/advisory-support/recommended-practices/recommended-practice-google-earth-engine-flood-mapping) (run it end to end in week 1); McFeeters, [The use of the Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI) in the delineation of open water features](https://doi.org/10.1080/01431169608948714) (1996).
- **2. Segmentation.** Why does a U-Net work well on small datasets, and what does a per-pixel IoU fail to tell you about a flood map's usefulness? *Read:* [U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597) (Ronneberger et al., 2015); the [Sen1Floods11](https://github.com/cloudtostreet/Sen1Floods11) dataset and its CVPR 2020 workshop paper (Bonafilia et al.).
- **3. Change detection and damage assessment.** Why compare before and after images rather than classify the after image alone, and what goes wrong when the two are misaligned? *Read:* [Fully Convolutional Siamese Networks for Change Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08462) (Daudt et al., 2018); [xBD: A Dataset for Assessing Building Damage from Satellite Imagery](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.09296) (Gupta et al., 2019).
- **4. Geospatial foundation models.** What does pretraining on unlabelled satellite imagery buy you when you only have a few hundred labelled chips? *Read:* [Foundation Models for Generalist Geospatial Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18660) (Prithvi; Jakubik et al., 2023), especially the flood-mapping experiments.
- **5. Working with geospatial data** (coordinate systems, rasters vs. vectors, reprojection, tiling, zonal statistics). How do you turn a flood raster and a population raster into "flooded people per district" without off-by-one-pixel or wrong-projection errors? *Read:* [Geocomputation with Python](https://py.geocompx.org/) (free online), chapters on raster and vector data and raster–vector interactions; the [Earth Engine guides](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides); [rasterio documentation](https://rasterio.readthedocs.io/).

## Text

- **6. Crisis text: classification and extraction.** What kinds of information do disaster reports and posts carry, and why do models trained on one disaster transfer poorly to the next? *Read:* [CrisisMMD: Multimodal Twitter Datasets from Natural Disasters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00713) (Alam et al., 2018); [HumAID: Human-Annotated Disaster Incidents Data from Twitter with Deep Learning Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.03090) (Alam et al., 2021).

## Combining signals

- **7. Multimodal fusion.** When should you fuse modalities early (shared features), late (separate predictions combined), or at the decision level (a scoring rule), and which survives a missing modality best? *Read:* [Multimodal Categorization of Crisis Events in Social Media](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.04917) (Abavisani et al., 2020); [Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00020) (CLIP; Radford et al., 2021) for how image and text get a shared space.
- **8. Composite indicators and vulnerability.** How do you build a score from many indicators that people can inspect and argue with, and how do you test how much the weights matter? *Read:* Cutter, Boruff and Shirley, [Social Vulnerability to Environmental Hazards](https://doi.org/10.1111/1540-6237.8402002) (2003); the [INFORM Risk Index](https://drmkc.jrc.ec.europa.eu/inform-index) methodology pages (a real, transparent humanitarian composite); the OECD/JRC *Handbook on Constructing Composite Indicators* (2008), chapters on weighting and sensitivity analysis; [A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07874) (SHAP; Lundberg and Lee, 2017) for the learned-model alternative.

## Measurement

- **9. Spatial validation and leakage.** Why does a random train/test split overstate accuracy for maps, and how do you split instead? *Read:* Roberts et al., [Cross-validation strategies for data with temporal, spatial, hierarchical, or phylogenetic structure](https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.02881) (2017); Ploton et al., [Spatial validation reveals poor predictive performance of large-scale ecological mapping models](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-18321-y) (2020).
- **10. Uncertainty, calibration and documentation.** What does it mean for a 0.8 flood probability to be trustworthy, and what should someone know about your data before using your ranking? *Read:* [On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599) (Guo et al., 2017); [Datasheets for Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010) (Gebru et al.).
